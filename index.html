Adidev Jhunjhunwala

I'm a researcher in the Creative Machines Lab at Columbia University, where I'm advised by Prof. Hod Lipson. I work on the wackier corners of AI and robot learning — how a machine builds, keeps and revises an internal picture of its own body as it learns, among other projects.

My interests run across reinforcement learning, continual and multi-task learning, interpretability and representation analysis, evolved morphology, and machine design. I care most about problems that don't sit inside one field, so if you're working on something at the edge of robotics and AI — or anywhere adjacent that's genuinely interesting — I'd like to hear about it.

SELECTED WORKS
R.01 Robot Self Awareness What, exactly, stays the same when a robot learns to do something new? Trained one policy across walk, wiggle and bob, then looked inside for a persistent self-like subnetwork. arXiv ↗
R.02 What does life look like in higher dimensions? Evolving soft-bodied robots in more than three spatial dimensions, and asking what locomotion means once the body has more room to move than the world does. YouTube ↗
E.01 GorrillaBot A bipedal robot with a servo-actuated hinge that became a quadruped in the last two days, when we realised grading was based purely on speed.
